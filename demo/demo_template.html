MRI AutoMeasure

RESEARCH DEMO Automatic pelvimetry & one-click tumour measurement for rectal cancer MRI — pilot, 10 patients

What this system does

It reproduces the standard rectal-cancer MRI measurements automatically. Every result shown in this demo comes from a model that never saw the patient it is measuring — each of the 10 pilot patients was measured by a model trained only on the other nine.

Input 1

The mid-sagittal T2 slice. Nothing else — no clicks.

→
Automatic

Nine anatomical points are located; inlet, outlet, depth and sacral angle are computed from them.

→
Output

Four pelvimetry values, within a few millimetres of yours.

Input 2

The axial slice plus one rough click on the tumour.

→
Automatic

The caliper endpoints for tumour and mesorectal dimensions are placed around your click.

→
Output

Tumour width/length, MRF width/length, CC extent — with honest confidence.

Why automate at all

When five of these patients were measured twice by the clinical team, the two readings disagreed — modestly for bone, substantially for soft tissue:

≤ 6 mm
largest difference between two readings of the same pelvimetry value (bone — very reproducible)
up to 30 mm
difference between two readings of the same tumour / MRF value (soft tissue — hard even for experts)
2.3 mm
this system's mean error on the pelvic depth, measured on patients it never saw

Honesty note: with only 8 training patients this tool is a proof of concept. Typical results land within the range where two expert readings of the same patient differ, but a minority of clicks produce clearly wrong output — you may find them. At ~300 patients the model retrains on thirty times the data, and unconfident predictions will be flagged for human measurement instead of shown.

How it was validated

Leave-one-out: the model never sees the patient it measures

With 10 pilot patients we train 10 separate models. Model 1 trains on patients 2–10 and is tested on patient 1; model 2 trains on 1 and 3–10 and is tested on patient 2; and so on. Every number in this demo is such a held-out test result — the strictest honest evaluation a 10-patient pilot allows.
