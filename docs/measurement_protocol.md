# MRI measurement protocol (public version)

Derived from a clinician's narrated demonstration, formalised for
reproducibility. Patient-specific notes are omitted from this public copy.

## Sequences and slices

- Sagittal measurements: the **mid-sagittal** T2 TSE slice (checked against
  the localizer), one slice for all sagittal measurements.
- Axial measurements: the T2 TSE axial slice where the **tumour has its
  largest horizontal diameter** (cross-checked on the localizer).

## Pelvimetry (mid-sagittal slice)

| Measurement | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| Pelvic inlet | Sacral promontory (anterior-superior corner of S1) | Superior margin of the pubic bone |
| Pelvic outlet | Dorsal/inferior margin of the pubic bone | Lowest bony point of the sacrum/coccyx |
| Pelvic depth | Sacral promontory | Same coccygeal point as the outlet |
| Sacral angulation | Cobb-style angle between two separately drawn segments: one along the anterior cortex of the upper sacrum, one along the lower sacrum/coccyx. The **obtuse** angle is recorded (viewers print `X°(180−X°)`). |

Note: adjacent measurements often share a clicked point (promontory, coccyx)
but are not guaranteed to — treat each caliper's endpoints independently.

## Tumour, sagittal

- **Craniocaudal extent**: highest to lowest point of the tumour (the
  intermediate-signal mass in the rectal wall). The line follows the local
  bowel axis and may approach horizontal for high/anterior tumours.
- **Height above the anorectal junction**: distance from the lower tumour
  border to the anorectal junction line (confirm junction vs. verge naming
  with your clinical team — they are different landmarks).

## Tumour and mesorectal package, axial

Two perpendicular calipers forming a cross, for each structure:

| Measurement | Direction |
|---|---|
| Tumour width / mesorectal (MRF) width | Transverse (left–right); the cross may be drawn oblique, following the mesorectal axis |
| Tumour length / mesorectal (MRF) length | Anteroposterior |

The mesorectal measurements span the whole fat package out to the
mesorectal fascia (the thin low-signal line enclosing it) — the TME
resection boundary.

## Reading annotated screenshots (if recovering ground truth)

- Match each printed value to its caliper via the thin leader line — label
  positions are not reliable and can appear visually swapped.
- Angle tools print value and supplement; distances are rounded to whole mm.
- "Blank" screenshots are not guaranteed to show the measured slice; verify
  by content, not by filename.
